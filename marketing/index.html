    01 Walk

    One reading, one tap, at a time.

    Command mode walks your superintendent through the building in the order they really walk it. One value, one tap — and a small buzz in the hand when it lands, so they never have to look twice.

    • Follows the building floor by floor, machine by machine
    • Add a reading and see exactly where it will appear
    • Works with a photo of the gauge or a typed value

    02 Snap

    Every photo carries its time and place.

    No gallery uploads, no “I did it yesterday.” Each photo is taken live, stamped with the moment and the GPS fix, and checked against the building it belongs to.

    03 Learn

    It learns what normal looks like.

    After three days it knows each gauge. A reading that drifts — 3° on a temperature, 5 PSI, 5% on a level — is questioned on the spot. And a typo like 1,000,000 never makes it into the record.

    04 Read

    Snap the paper schedule. The AI builds the rest.

    Photo, PDF or pasted text in. A week grid out — one row per task, the way the paper looked. Duplicates are merged, so you check a clean sheet, not a mess.

    Check one grid

    Assign

    05 Prove

    A task isn’t done until the photos are in.

    Cleaners check a task off, take one wide shot and the close-ups you require — and only then can they finish it. You see who did it, when, and where they stood.

    • You choose the photo count — per schedule or per task
    • Enforce the time window, or leave it as a guide
    • Late finishes are recorded as late, not hidden
